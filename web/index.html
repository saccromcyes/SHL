      How accurate is it?

      The recommender is tested on 28 hiring requests, each labelled by hand with the catalog assessments a recruiter would expect. Recall@10 is the share of those expected assessments that appear in the top ten; MAP@10 also rewards ranking them higher. Each row adds one stage of the pipeline.
